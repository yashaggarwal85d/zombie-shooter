## Gaps and uncertainty

- The comparables are mostly hits (selection bias). S41 and S42 give the base rate for typical outcomes. Newer 2025 median figures (about $250–$1,000) exist only from secondary aggregators and are not used.
- No reliable public sales figures exist for most comparables. Review counts are the main proxy, and no review-to-sales multiplier was used.
- Genre-specific wishlist conversion data is behind GameDiscoverCo's paywall.
- The tax treatment of Steam income for an Indian resident needs a chartered accountant's confirmation.
- Premium mobile market data is from 2022 and comes from one analyst; it needs refreshing before any mobile investment.
- All hour and revenue estimates in the report are my own, labelled as estimates.
