# Asset Register

Every third-party or self-made asset used in the project, with where it came from and its licence. An asset can't go into the game until it has a row here.

**Licence rules (DECISION_LOG D4):** CC0 preferred. CC-BY is allowed with attribution in the credits. Nothing non-commercial (NC), personal-use only, "free for any use" without a written licence, or AI-generated.

**Status values:** `placeholder` (prototype only, will be replaced) · `final` (ships in the game).
